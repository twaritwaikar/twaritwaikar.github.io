**G for Golf** is a golf game with a twist. The player controls the ball's gravity instead of the ball, which means full control in 3D space. Made and polished in Unreal Engine 4.

I also developed a **VR edition** of the game (leading 2 more members in my team) which won prizes at the [Microsoft Code.Fun.Do 2018](https://www.facebook.com/cfdiitr/posts/pfbid0Lr2VKYN4tf1E2b5xV8wfzmPyzhUPJYu9bY2eTqo2R7zMZxciEH3gsF3cAPEGRNDAl) hackathon and the 'Srishti 2018' exhibition at IIT Roorkee.

The game shipped with 3 levels, each exploring a different design possibility in this idea.

## Main features

- Bright, colourful music and ambience
- Unique gameplay with a simple control scheme
- Special VR edition

## Tech stack

- Unreal Engine Blueprints
- Unreal Engine 4

Created during SDS Winter of Code 2017 under mentorship from SDS members.

**G for Golf** is a golf game with a twist. The player controls the ball's gravity instead of the ball, which means full control in 3D space.

The VR edition won prizes at multiple hackathons and exhibitions.

The game shipped with 3 levels, each exploring a different design possibility in this idea.

## Main features

- Special VR edition
- Bright, colourful music and ambience
- Unique gameplay with a simple interface

## Tech stack

- Unreal Engine Blueprints
- Unreal Engine 4

Created during SDS Winter of Code 2017 under mentorship from SDS members.

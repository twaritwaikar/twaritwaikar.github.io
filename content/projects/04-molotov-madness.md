**Molotov Madness** is a 3D arcade game built using Godot Engine, where you have to iradicate pests from your customers' homes in space.

Use WASD to move and hold left-click to charge up a molotov cocktail shot. The more you charge it up, the higher and faster it goes.

I was fortunate enough to work with my talented friends, who helped take this game to a well-polished state by the time our hackathon submission window ended (48 hours).

## Main features

- Stylistic visuals with hard metal music going in the background
- Addictive, arcade-y gameplay with instant replayability
- Several rendering and gameplay feel tricks are in use to make the game look more ["juiced up"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg)

## Tech stack

- Godot Engine 4.2

Created during the WTF x IGDC Game Jam 2024.

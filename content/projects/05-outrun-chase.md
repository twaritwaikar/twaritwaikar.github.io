**Outrun Chase** is a top down racing shooter game built with Godot Engine with unique grinding mechanics lifted from [Datawing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.DanVogt.DATAWING&hl=en_IN) which are then put in a retro stylistic sense and multiplayer deathmatches/racing feature set.

Use WASD to move and grind along the edges of the track to build up your power level. Once the power level is full you can unleash a cannon to stun opponents for a few seconds.

Outrun Chase was later adopted by [SDSLabs](sdslabs.co) as a main product offering and was ported to Android/iOS devices as well using the Godot Engine. My sister remembers playing this game with her friends over the internet using IPv6.

## Main features

- Retro art style and juiced up gameplay feel for immersion
- Multiplayer P2P functionality across 4 players without servers.
- Featured on [Godot Con 2021 Flash talks](https://youtu.be/CrOI5HUAPCg?t=7991)

## Tech stack

- Godot Engine 4

Created during the HAB 2021 Hackathon.
